# Approved coverage exceptions

The 100% line-and-branch requirement applies to new or materially changed unit-testable implementation. The following are narrowly approved defensive or external-boundary exceptions, not permission to leave reachable behaviour untested. Reassess an exception when its code, supported operating model or Roslyn dependency changes. Do not introduce fake Roslyn runtime objects, reflection calls or production test hooks to force impossible states.

## Control-flow resolution

`ControlFlowGraphResolver` derives the enclosing symbol from the resolved node and semantic model. Supported executable roots, local functions, lambdas and standalone parameter-initialiser graphs are covered through real Roslyn objects. Nullable nested-graph results and future unsupported operation roots remain defensive external-API guards; they do not justify replacing Roslyn with artificial objects solely to raise a percentage.

## Workspace guards

`WorkspaceResolver` retains defensive missing-semantic-model and missing-compilation alternatives after successful supported C# resolution, including compiler-lowered async conditions. `WorkspaceDiffBuilder` retains the Roslyn `SolutionChanges` consistency guard for a change ID whose document cannot be resolved. These do not arise from a valid supported solution graph; reassess them if that invariant changes.

## Host integration boundaries

Actual process bootstrap, MSBuild locator discovery/registration failures and external registration races, real recovery-file loading, and DI/MCP composition belong to integration or published-Host tests. This applies to `Program`, `MsBuildRegistrationService`, `RecoveryStatusReader` and `RoslynWorkbenchHostApplicationBuilderExtensions`; orchestration and ordinary branches that can be isolated remain unit-test responsibilities.

Real MEF and assembly-load-context behaviour in plugin discovery is an integration boundary. It does not exempt `PluginCatalogLoader` orchestration, candidate preparation, collision handling or materialisation from unit tests. Defensive assembly-version fallbacks in `ServerStatusService` and SDK schema-exporter compatibility paths in `ToolSchemaBuilder` remain approved where the supported unit surface cannot produce the alternative.
